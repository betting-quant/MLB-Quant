## Discrepancies found and corrected

The initial audit found four transformation issues before the final comparison:

1. **All 20 home/away/team labels were reversed.** Statcast `Top` means the away
   team is batting, so the pitcher is the home team; `Bottom` means the pitcher
   is the away team. The mapping was corrected.
2. **Luis Gil and Alec Marsh were each short one out.** Their Statcast rows had
   `sac_fly` terminal events that were missing from the event-out map. Sacrifice
   flies now count as one out.
3. **Keaton Winn was short one out.** One runner out was absent from the pitch
   rows, but the pitcher resumed in a later inning and the completed prior half
   inning was short of three explicit event outs. Completed-inning reconciliation
   now accounts for that missing runner play.
4. **Shota Imanaga had one excess pitch.** The extra Statcast row was an
   `automatic_ball`, which is not an official thrown pitch. It is excluded from
   pitches thrown.
5. **Joe Musgrove had one excess batter faced.** A `truncated_pa` marker appeared
   within an at-bat that is present in pitch data but absent from the official BF
   total. The entire marked at-bat is now excluded from batters faced.

After these corrections, the final independent comparison was 20/20 for every
requested metric. No additional MLB seasons were downloaded.

## Recommendation

**SAFE TO SCALE**, with the existing audit controls retained and rerun after any
major ingestion or transformation change. The raw pitch data remains immutable;
the processed table was regenerated from the cached raw Parquet after correction.
